HourSnap

Your day,
one hour at a time.

Every hour you snap what you see and your face at the same time. Pick your own hours for school days, weekends and holidays. Snaps vanish after 24 hours.

Download for Android
  1. Open the downloaded HourSnap.apk
  2. If your phone asks, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source
  3. Tap Install, open HourSnap, type your name and pick a PIN
Or use the web app
Open HourSnap
  1. Open this page in Safari
  2. Tap Share then Add to Home Screen
  3. Open HourSnap from your home screen, type your name and pick a PIN

No App Store needed. It works like a real app.
